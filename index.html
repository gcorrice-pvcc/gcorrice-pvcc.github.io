My topic is if it is acceptable to participate in today’s technical society without making an effort to understand the basics of that technology. Although my opinion is that it is not acceptable, as a society that relies on a technology will not function if its members do not understand the technology, a differing opinion would be that it is acceptable under the circumstances that those individuals not directly using a technology do not need to be aware of it. Another opinion would be that this is not feasible due to so many technologies existing, that being expected to understand all of them is unrealistic.
